Delete Your TotoQuest Account

For the TotoQuest game on the web and the TotoQuest Android app · October 2, 2026

You can delete your TotoQuest account and its data at any time.

Delete it in the game (fastest)

  1. Open TotoQuest and sign in.
  2. Tap your picture in the bottom-left corner to open your profile.
  3. Tap Settings, then Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. If your account has a password, enter it.

This happens straight away.

Or ask us to delete it

Can't open the game? Email totoquestofficial@yahoo.com with the subject "Delete my TotoQuest account". Include your username and the email address you signed up with. We'll delete your account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your trainer, Totos, items, candy, gems and settings saved on your device.
  • Your leaderboard entry (username, character and stats) and your player record on our online service.
  • Your password (it's only ever stored on your device, scrambled).

What we keep

  • Records of purchases (order ID, pack, amount and date), which tax and accounting laws require us to keep for up to 7 years. They aren't linked to your username once your account is deleted.

Candy or gems you bought can't be restored after you delete your account. See the Return and Refund Policy and the Privacy Policy for more.
