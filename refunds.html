Return and Refund Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

The TotoQuest store sells digital packs of candy and gems. They're delivered straight into the game as soon as your payment goes through, so they can't be "returned" like a physical item. We still want every purchase to feel fair, so here's when we'll give your money back.

The short version
  • Didn't get what you paid for? We'll deliver it or refund you in full.
  • Bought by mistake, or a child bought without permission? Ask within 14 days for a full refund.
  • Otherwise, purchases are final, except where the law says otherwise.

Time limits count from the date of purchase shown on your receipt. When we refund a purchase, we may remove the matching candy or gems from the game.

How to ask for a refund

Email totoquestofficial@yahoo.com with:

  • the email address you used at checkout;
  • the date of the purchase and the pack you bought (for example, "500 Candy");
  • the receipt or order number from your receipt email, if you have it;
  • your TotoQuest username and a short note about what happened.

We usually reply within 3 business days.

Your purchase is processed by Stripe, whose Link service is the reseller (merchant of record) for TotoQuest purchases. You can also ask for help through the support link in your receipt email from Link. Stripe may issue refunds directly in some cases.

When you'll get your money

Approved refunds go back to the payment method you used, for the full amount you paid, including tax. Stripe processes them right away. Depending on your bank, the money usually shows up within 5–10 business days.

Chargebacks

If something's wrong, please contact us first. We can usually fix it faster than a bank dispute. If a purchase is charged back, we may remove the matching candy or gems and limit future purchases on that account.

Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy takes away rights you have under the consumer laws where you live. If those laws give you a longer refund period or other rights, they apply.
