    Joining the lobby…
    How PvP works • 4 vs 4: your team of 4 Totos against another trainer’s. Trainers take turns: on your turn pick ONE of your Totos, then ATTACK or one of its abilities, then who to hit. • Attackers hit hardest and charge a big attack after 4 hits. Tanks Taunt (your rival has to hit them for 3 turns), guard and heal a little. Healers keep the team alive. Supporters weaken rivals and boost your team. • Abilities need a few turns to cool down — when they aren’t ready, use your normal ATTACK. • Win vs a trainer +25 RP, lose −15. Win vs a computer +10, lose −5. Every battle gives Gold and Food. • Ranks: Bronze 3 → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond 1 → Master (1,500 RP). The top 10 Masters are Grandmasters. • The season resets on the 1st of every month. Finish at #1 to win the Rank #1 Champion Toto (Class S+) for good. The #1 trainer on the PvE board wins the PvE Rank #1 Champion.

    Support
    Players: tap someone in Users for everything you can do: gifts, take-backs, rank changes, bans and unbans (every one asks you to confirm first).
    Gifts & take-backs (owner) reach the player the next time their game is open, usually within a minute. A take-back never goes below zero: taking 1,000,000 gems takes all they have.
    Rank: “Set season Rank Points” lowers or raises a rank (0 = Bronze 3, 1,500+ = Master); “Reset rank” puts them back to Bronze 3.
    Trades: players can only trade with friends, and Totos earned from achievements (Rank Champions, their partner Toni) can’t be traded.
    Reports are about players: a message someone reported (you see it with the chat around it) or a player reported from “Report a problem”. Tap ⚖️ Take action to mute them, ban them (you choose how long), remove the message, or take no action. Muting or banning also removes the reported message from the chat.
    Content is bugs, glitches, store problems and anything else players report, with their description and their game version and device. Mark them handled once they’re dealt with.
    Muted players can still play but can’t send chat messages until the mute ends.
